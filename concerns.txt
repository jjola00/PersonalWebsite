3. /api/spotify/token IS A PUBLIC TOKEN MINTER                       [security]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/app/api/spotify/token/route.js
Unauthenticated POST, no rate limit. Returns a live Spotify access token for
your account to anyone who calls it. Low blast radius (read scopes only) but
it is an open endpoint. Fix: call it server-side only, or gate + rate limit.

Related, same area:
  src/app/api/auth/spotify/callback/route.js
  One-off dev helper that renders a refresh token into raw HTML. Ships to
  production. Links to /test-spotify, a route that does not exist (404).
  Should be deleted or dev-gated.
